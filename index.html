My Creative Journey

I began animating by filling the margins of my school notebooks with flipbook sketches. That playful curiosity evolved into a career directing animated commercials, music videos, and cinematic shorts seen across millions of screens globally.

Workflow & Philosophy

Every frame should either push the narrative forward or spark genuine delight. Whether rigging puppets in Toon Boom or hand-keying high-octane anime sakuga, I treat animation as visual music where timing, anticipation, and release create magic.
